I'm Denis V. Aleksandrov, an **[Adobe Certified Professional - Adobe Commerce Developer](https://certification.adobe.com/credential/verify/a197b9d4-a7d1-11f1-bdd6-42010a400002)** based in Skopje, North Macedonia. I work remotely with agencies, merchants, and development teams that need a dependable Magento 2 developer who can take ownership of both development work and production problems.  

My background combines 6 years of hands-on Magento/e-commerce experience (2019-2024), 7 years of project-management experience (2011-2018), and an IT career spanning more than 25 years since 2000.  

My Magento work has included custom modules, upgrades, checkout customization, REST APIs, payment and search integrations, Docker development environments, production defect resolution, malware investigation and remediation, localization, cron jobs, plugins, observers, shipping functionality, and Magento Admin customization.  

From 2019 to 2021, I worked on Magento projects at CodeWell Unlimited. Among them were [Zazu](https://web.archive.org/web/20220416060149/https://codewellgroup.com/zazu), an e-commerce and children's-event platform, and [Kniga (Book)](https://web.archive.org/web/20220416060153/https://codewellgroup.com/kniga), a Magento-based ecosystem for printed books and eBooks. My work on Kniga included payment gateway and Firebase integrations, custom REST APIs, shipping and cash-on-delivery functionality, cron jobs, plugins, observers, localization, admin configuration and frontend customization.  

From February to June 2023, I worked remotely as a Magento 2 developer for NTT DATA Spain on the Orange Belgium e-commerce solution. I developed modules from user stories, fixed defects in staging and production, worked extensively on bulk order-status-update functionality, integrated Algolia, and customized the one-page checkout.  

From November 2023 to August 2024, I worked remotely for Syncit Group, upgrading production Magento stores, building Docker environments, investigating and removing malware, and customizing Magento's standard checkout.  

In the meantime, I was studying for the [AD0-E724 certification "Adobe Certified Professional - Adobe Commerce Developer"](https://certification.adobe.com/certification/adobe-commerce-developer-professional-v2/1242). To achieve that, I enrolled in the [SwiftOtter](https://learning.swiftotter.com/) and [M.academy](https://m.academy/) schools. I even sparked a good discussion on [Campus](https://campus.m.academy/), the premium community for Magento developers, which led Mark Shust to write an excellent article: [The Unexpected Behavior of Magento Plugin Execution Order](https://m.academy/articles/magento-plugin-execution-order/).    

What distinguishes my background from that of many developers is that I also spent more than seven years in project management. I understand that successful software delivery involves more than writing code: requirements must be clarified, risks communicated, priorities understood, problems followed through, and people kept informed. My earlier IT career also includes software development, QA, technical support, system administration, teaching and embedded systems.  

In 2026, I earned the [Adobe Certified Professional - Adobe Commerce Developer](https://certification.adobe.com/credential/verify/a197b9d4-a7d1-11f1-bdd6-42010a400002) certification. I continue studying Magento because I want to be the person a team can turn to when they need someone who understands both the platform and the wider problem around it.
